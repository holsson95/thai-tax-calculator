# Home page (/) surface brief

Scope: the front page of mythaitaxes.com. Mode: Persuade. Later the world rolls out to the rest of the site (header, footer, article, calculator and info pages).

Audience: English-speaking foreigners in Thailand and residents remitting foreign income. Action: type an income and get a tax estimate in the first viewport, then choose the deeper calculator. Proof: Revenue Department sources and tax-year labels on every figure; 2026 bracket table and deductions summary stay on the page. Constraints: no tax engine changes; estimates labelled; no imitation of official RD forms or emblems; ad slot kept below the fold.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a fanned stack of coloured carbon-copy sheets, one per calculator; the top sheet is a working form that estimates tax as you type. It refuses the category default of a headline-over-card fintech hero with a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ultramarine desk (#344A9B family) as page ground; carbonless copy papers in white, canary, pink and sky blue as the major surfaces; ultramarine form ruling, numbered field labels in small tracked caps, boxed digit cells in a mono face; red rubber-stamp ink reserved only for the live result; perforation lines as dividers; tab edges as navigation. Raises: provenance tickets on every figure (bazaar); the working form is always the nearest sheet (multiplane); one state vocabulary: filled box entered, struck line not applicable, stamp is the result (one-bit); monthly 12-month row never wraps (step row); tab edge is the summary, pulling forward is the detail (toy box); stamp red only for the result (poster).

STORY: Visitor understands this is a free Thai tax estimator for people like them, sees a number within seconds, trusts it because the source and year sit beside it, then pulls the sheet for their situation (annual, monthly, foreign income) or tears off to the reference below.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height ultramarine desk. Left 40%: wordmark in nav, big white display headline, one-paragraph promise. Right 60%: four fanned sheets with tabs Quick estimate / Annual return / Monthly withholding / Foreign income; the white top sheet holds period checkboxes, income input in boxed cells, social security checkbox, the tax-due box and a red ESTIMATE stamp that lands when a result exists, plus a source ticket. Clicking a tab brings that sheet forward with its summary and link. Bottom edge: perforated "tear here" strip with a down arrow that scrolls to the reference.

FORM: Carbon-copy form set, position 4 on the grounded list; seed:b8accb6f4b55300f
