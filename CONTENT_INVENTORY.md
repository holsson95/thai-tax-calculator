Recommend rewriting each with a worked example/table, or consolidating into the newer pillar articles that already cover the same ground more thoroughly.

- **Retirement cluster** (4 articles) and **freelancer cluster** (7 articles) are internally differentiated by sub-topic (visa vs. investment vs. pension income; VAT vs. WHT vs. record-keeping) rather than true duplicates — no action needed beyond ensuring cross-links exist instead of restating shared background (LTR visa details, 180-day rule) in each one.
- **Country-specific expat guides** (UK, US, and the general expat guide) are the most original content on the site — each cites primary foreign-country sources (HMRC, IRS, FinCEN) in addition to Thai RD sources and are not duplicative of each other.
- **FAQ page** (43 Q&As, 8 categories) is not individually sourced per answer — worth a pass to add source links or cross-references to the relevant article for any FAQ answer stating a specific rate, threshold, or rule.
