How this calculator works

This calculator walks through Thailand's annual personal income tax return step by step: your employment type, gross income, and eligible deductions and allowances. It applies Thailand's progressive tax brackets to your taxable income and shows a full breakdown of what you owe, with the option to download a PDF summary for your records. Everything runs in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Who should use this calculator?
Anyone filing a Thai PND 90/91 return: salaried employees, freelancers, sole proprietors, and company owners, including expats with foreign income remitted to Thailand.
Does it account for deductions and allowances?
Yes. The form walks through standard personal allowances, spouse and dependent allowances, social security contributions, and common deductions before applying the tax brackets.
Is my data saved anywhere?
Your answers are kept only in your browser's session storage so you can resume if you navigate away. Nothing is sent to a server unless you choose to request a specialist review or email yourself a breakdown.
