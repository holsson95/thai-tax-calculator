How this site is funded

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The calculators and guides are free

You can use every calculator and article on My Thai Taxes without paying or signing up. The calculators run in your browser; nothing you type into them is sent to us unless you choose to request a specialist review or email yourself a breakdown.

Two ways we earn money

  • Advertising. Some pages show ads served by Google AdSense. See the privacy policy for how ad cookies work.
  • Referral fees. After a calculation you can ask for a free call with a partner tax firm. If you do, we may receive a referral fee from that firm, either per qualified enquiry or as a share of their fee. You never pay us anything, and the fee does not change what the firm charges you.

How we keep the numbers independent

  • Partner firms have no say in our calculations, tax rules or articles.
  • Every tax rate and rule the calculators use is listed with its source on our sources page, and our method is on the methodology page.
  • We show the specialist-review option more prominently when a result involves things the calculator cannot settle (for example LTR visas, mixed savings and income, or capital gains) or when the estimated tax is higher. It is never shown in place of the result.
  • We only share your details with a partner firm if you tick the consent box naming that firm.

Not tax advice

Our calculators give estimates for information only. Partner firms are independent; any advice you receive comes from them, not from My Thai Taxes. Questions: info@mythaitaxes.com.
