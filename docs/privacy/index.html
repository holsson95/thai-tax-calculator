Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Who we are

This website (mythaitaxes.com) provides free tools and informational articles to help individuals estimate their Thai personal income tax. It is operated independently. For questions about this policy, contact info@mythaitaxes.com.

2. Data you enter into the calculators

When you use the Annual Tax Calculator or Monthly Withholding Estimator, the income figures, deduction amounts, and other financial details you enter are stored only in your browser's session storage — a temporary area that is cleared automatically when you close the browser tab or click "Start Over." This data is never transmitted to our servers, never stored in a database, and never shared with anyone. We do not see it.

Because all calculation happens locally in your browser, your financial information stays on your device unless you choose to request a specialist review or email yourself a breakdown (sections 4 and 5). Only then is a summary of your result sent, together with the details you enter in that form.

3. Cookies and tracking technologies

We do not set any first-party cookies ourselves. However, the third-party services listed below place their own cookies and collect usage data when you visit this site.

Google Analytics (GA4)

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — which pages are visited, how long visitors stay, and where traffic comes from. Google Analytics sets cookies that identify your browser across sessions. The data is aggregated and does not personally identify you to us. Google may process this data on servers outside your country.

  • To opt out: install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Google Privacy Policy

Google AdSense

We display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites. These cookies allow Google and its partners to show you ads that may be relevant to your interests. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time.

  • Opt out of personalised ads: Google Ad Settings
  • Opt out via industry tool: aboutads.info
  • How Google uses advertising cookies

Ahrefs Analytics

We use Ahrefs Analytics to measure site traffic and search performance. Ahrefs collects anonymised page view data. See the Ahrefs Privacy Policy for details.

4. Specialist review requests

After a calculation you can ask for a free call with a partner tax firm. If you submit that form we collect: your name, email, optional WhatsApp/phone number, nationality, days in Thailand, income types, approximate yearly remittance range, urgency, any notes you add, a summary of your calculator result (estimated tax, remitted and taxable amounts, residency and complexity flags), the page you were on, how you arrived (referrer and campaign/UTM tags) and a record of your consent (the exact wording and the time).

We share these details with the partner tax firm named on the form only if you tick the consent box naming them. The box is never pre-ticked and the form cannot be sent without it. We use the details to pass on your request, to send you a confirmation email, and to keep a record of referrals for invoicing the partner firm. The partner firm then handles your data under its own privacy policy. We may receive a referral fee: how this site is funded.

Legal basis: your consent (Thailand's PDPA section 19 and, where it applies, GDPR article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us; this does not affect what was shared before you withdrew, and we will ask the partner firm to stop contacting you.

5. "Email me my breakdown" and updates

If you ask us to email your tax breakdown, we use your email address to send it and keep a record that we did. Separately, and only if you tick the (unticked by default) box, we will send you occasional Thai tax updates. Every update includes an unsubscribe link. Your email is not shared with partner firms through this feature.

6. Service providers and international transfers

  • Cloudflare hosts the form service and database, and provides spam protection (Turnstile).
  • Resend sends the confirmation, lead and breakdown emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website itself.

These providers, and our operating company, may process data outside Thailand, including in the United States. We rely on the providers' contractual safeguards for these transfers.

7. Exchange rate lookups

When you enter foreign income in the Annual Tax Calculator or the Foreign Income Tax Calculator, the site may fetch the Bank of Thailand's published daily exchange rate for the currency and date you specify. This request is made through a proxy server to comply with browser security restrictions. No personal or financial data from your form is included in this request — only the currency code and date.

8. Information this site does not collect

  • We do not collect your name or contact details unless you submit one of the forms above or email us
  • We do not have user accounts or logins
  • We do not store your detailed calculator inputs on any server; only the result summary described in section 4 is sent, and only if you submit the form
  • We do not sell data. Sharing with a partner firm happens only with your explicit consent
  • Analytics events do not include your name, email, phone number, notes or exact amounts

9. Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and, where it applies, the EU GDPR, you can ask us for access to the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy, and withdraw consent. You can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee. For data held by Google or Ahrefs, use the opt-out links above or contact them directly.

For any privacy enquiries, email info@mythaitaxes.com.

10. Data retention

Specialist review requests are kept for 24 months after the last update to their status (needed for referral invoicing and disputes), then deleted. Email-capture records are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, and breakdown-only records for 24 months. Calculator data in session storage is deleted when you close the browser tab. Google Analytics retains event data for 14 months by default (configurable in the GA4 console). AdSense cookies persist for up to 13 months. We do not control the retention policies of third-party services.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if we add new features or services. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page will reflect any changes.

12. Contact

Questions or concerns about this privacy policy can be sent to info@mythaitaxes.com.
